Free · No account · Works offline

A tracker that does not lie to you about your own data.

Steady follows your habits, training, sleep and recovery, and keeps every bit of it on your phone. No sign-up, no server, nothing uploaded. When it does not know something, it says so rather than filling the gap with a number that looks convincing.

Open the app See the code

Add it to your home screen and it behaves like any other app, including with no signal.

What it actually does

Six screens, no clutter, and every section can be switched off if you do not want it.

📊

Today

Recovery, effort and sleep as three readable rings, with the reasoning behind each one a tap away.

✅

Habits

Your own habits, your own words. Streaks that do not break just because the day is not over yet.

🏋️

Training

Gym sets with real personal records, plus running, swimming, rope, football, padel, climbing and anything else.

⚖️

Body

Weight with a smoothed trend that survives a heavy morning, plus water, food and sleep.

📈

Progress

A weekly review that judges the days that have happened, badges you cannot fake, and a journal.

🕌

Prayers

Optional. The five daily prayers with times for your location and a ledger for missed ones.

Where the numbers come from

Every score is an estimate built from published, citable method. None of it copies a vendor's private algorithm, and none of it is medical advice.

Score Method What it uses
Recovery HRV-guided readiness, after Plews and Buchheit Your own 30-day baselines for HRV and resting heart rate, plus sleep
Effort Banister TRIMP Time spent in each heart-rate zone against your heart-rate reserve
Fitness age Nes and the HUNT study norms Measured VO₂max, or the Jackson non-exercise estimate
Sleep need Your own baseline plus accumulated debt Nightly duration, capped so one bad week cannot distort it

A missing input is never counted as zero. If a component has no data behind it, it is dropped and the rest are re-weighted, so a score is always built from things that actually happened.

A day in progress is not a day you failed. The weekly review measures the days that have happened, not a full seven, so Tuesday does not grade you as if you had already lost the week.

A gap in a chart is a gap. A day you did not weigh yourself is not a day you weighed nothing, and the chart will not draw a cliff that never happened.

Your data stays yours

There is no account because there is no server. Everything is stored in your browser, on your device.

🔒

Nothing is uploaded

No analytics, no tracking, no third party receives anything you enter.

💾

Export whenever

One tap writes a JSON file you own. Restoring shows you what is in it before replacing anything, and keeps an undo.

✈️

Works with no signal

The whole app is one file. Nothing needs a connection except optional prayer times.

The flip side of storing nothing on a server: clearing your browser data, or losing the phone, loses your history. Export a backup now and then.

Steady is free and open source. Read the code, or take it and run your own copy.
